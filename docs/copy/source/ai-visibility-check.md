# Free AI visibility check

See what ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and Gemini say about your brand today. We record exactly what we see and send your five most useful fixes within 3 working days. No guarantees, just a clear picture.

**[Get a free AI visibility check](#request-a-check)**

No guarantees, just a clear picture. Limited spots each week. We send results within 3 working days of a complete intake.

## What you get

A free AI visibility check is an observed snapshot of how ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and Gemini describe your brand and category, on a set date, plus the five fixes that matter most. We record what we see. We don't promise a mention, a citation or a ranking, and answers can change.

This is a human-run snapshot, not a live scanner or a self-serve tool. For each check we send a simple scorecard of what we observed on that date (it isn't a ranking), your five most useful fixes and the prompt log, so you can see exactly what we asked and what came back. Local businesses also get a basic look at their public Google Business Profile and how they compare with the top local results. The deliverable is a short report or a short recorded walkthrough, plus that prompt log. The report is the usual default.

## Is my business on ChatGPT?

The only honest answer is to look. We run a fixed set of prompts about your brand, category and competitors on ChatGPT, and on Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and Gemini, then record whether you are named or cited. A miss is an observation, not a verdict, because answers vary by time, place and settings.

If you want to work on citations yourself first, start with our guide on [how to get your brand cited by ChatGPT](/guides/how-to-get-your-brand-cited-by-chatgpt).

## Why a brand might not show up on ChatGPT

There isn't one cause. AI answers vary by prompt, location, account and date, and no platform can promise a mention. Common gaps we look for are a site the crawlers can't use, a thin or inconsistent brand story and competitors who are cited more often than you. The check reports what we observed, then the five fixes that matter most.

## What's included, and what isn't

What's included is an observed snapshot on ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and Gemini. We record the date, the mode we used and the location we tested from. You also get the five fixes that matter most, and a short report or a short recorded walkthrough plus the prompt log. We can't check every mode or every future model.

What's not included is any login or account access, a guaranteed mention, citation or ranking, or a free re-check. The check uses public information only. We will not ask for passwords or account access. What we see on a public Google Business Profile is what the public sees that day. We can't see verification status or account settings.

The free check is a single snapshot. Re-checks are for clients, using the same prompt set so you can compare later. If you want ongoing work on search and AI answers, the usual next step is a short, no-pressure fit call about the [Authority Kit](/kits/authority-kit), or the [Local Domination Kit](/kits/local-domination-kit) for local businesses. You can also take [SEO/AEO/GEO](/services/seo-aeo-geo) on its own.

## How the check works, and how long it takes

We send your results within 3 working days of a complete intake, if a spot is available. There are limited spots each week, so we can do each check properly. If we're full, we'll say when the next spot is free. The 3 working days start when the form is complete, not when you first click send.
