## Capacity flag
At 3 guides a week (roughly 1,200 to 1,800 words each), on top of daily LinkedIn copy and website pages, Lila's load roughly doubles. AI speeds up the research and first drafts, and every guide is still edited by a person and QA'd by me. If quality slips, I'll recommend dropping to 2 a week after the first month rather than publishing thin guides.
