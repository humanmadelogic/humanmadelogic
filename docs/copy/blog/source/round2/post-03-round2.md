## 5. Visible FAQ (Q and A pairs, or 'none')

Place `## Questions people ask` after the CTA block and immediately before `## Sources`. Add FAQPage markup only for these four Q&As, word for word.

**Are ads in Google AI Mode live in Australia, Canada and Singapore?**
Google said on 28 October 2026 that it is scaling ads in AI Mode in 10 new countries, including Australia, Canada and Singapore.

**Are AI Mode ads live in the UK?**
Not according to Google. Its 28 October post says it plans to expand to additional locales in Q4 2026, including the United Kingdom. That is a plan, not a launch.

**Which Google Ads campaigns can show in AI Mode?**
Google's Ads Help page says existing Search, Shopping and Performance Max campaigns are automatically eligible to serve ads within AI Overviews and AI Mode. It also says these ads are available in English in the United States and select English-speaking markets, so check what applies to your own account and market.

**Do ads change Google's AI answers?**
Google's Ads Help page says paid advertising does not influence the organic responses generated by Google AI.
