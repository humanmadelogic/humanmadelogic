## 5. Visible FAQ (Q and A pairs, or 'none')

Place `## Questions people ask` after the CTA block and immediately before `## Sources`. Add FAQPage markup only for these four Q&As, word for word.

**Can staff write reviews of their own company's products?**
The CMA says commissioning reviews isn't limited to paying consumers, and that unlawful conduct could include a business requesting members of staff to write reviews about its products or services.

**What does the CMA expect on prices and discounts?**
The CMA says discounts must be genuine and businesses must show the total price clearly and throughout the purchasing journey, without opting customers in by pre-selecting extra services.

**When do the new UK subscription rules start?**
The CMA says a new subscription contracts regime will come into force in January, and that the government will publish secondary legislation and further guidance ahead of it. The CMA also warns businesses not to assume that its support for compliance means enforcement is being delayed.

**Can a short ad skip the basis of a green claim?**
Not according to the ASA. In its ProCook ruling it said an ad with a 270-character limit was not so limited that the basis of a carbon neutral claim couldn't be made clear.
