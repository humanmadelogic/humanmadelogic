## 5. Visible FAQ (Q and A pairs, or 'none')

Place `## Questions people ask` after the CTA block and immediately before `## Sources`. Add FAQPage markup only for these four Q&As, word for word.

**Can I claim a ChatGPT business profile outside the US?**
OpenAI says any eligible business located where ChatGPT is available can claim their business through Business Hub, and that availability depends on the business's location and type. It doesn't list eligible countries or categories. One catch: local stores and restaurants can currently claim only an existing US listing.

**Is Business Hub the same as the ChatGPT Business plan?**
No. This is not ChatGPT's paid Business plan. OpenAI's help page says you don't need an Ads account or a ChatGPT Enterprise agreement to claim an eligible business.

**Does claiming a profile make ChatGPT recommend you more?**
OpenAI says it doesn't guarantee increased visibility or give you direct control over ChatGPT's independent recommendations, and that advertising doesn't influence ChatGPT's independent answers.

**What do Business Hub mentions count?**
For an online business, a mention counts when ChatGPT references its website. Naming the brand alone doesn't count. Insights show aggregate activity over the past 28 days.
