**What are the main consumer trends for 2027?**
The 2027 reports we read point the same way: beauty is merging with wellness, shoppers want proof over a famous name, AI is starting to shape the shortlist and trust still comes from people.

**How much does AI influence beauty purchases?**
Euromonitor's beauty survey says generative AI already sways 8% of colour cosmetics purchasers and 15% of Beauty Innovators. Its global trends research says only 19% see GenAI as a highly trustworthy source of shopping information.

**Do beauty shoppers still buy in store?**
McKinsey's Alexis Wolfer says more than two-thirds of beauty sales globally, and about 50 percent in the US, still happen in store.

**What do AI tools look for in beauty products?**
McKinsey partner Sara Hudson says LLMs pick up on functional words: how something is used, what ingredients are in it and what ingredients are not.
