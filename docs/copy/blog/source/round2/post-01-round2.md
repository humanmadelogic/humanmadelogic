## 5. Visible FAQ (Q and A pairs, or 'none')

Place `## Questions people ask` after the CTA block and immediately before `## Sources`. Add FAQPage markup only for these four Q&As, word for word.

**When did Google's November 2026 core update start?**
Google's Search Status Dashboard says it released the November 2026 core update on 10 November. It applies globally and to all languages, and the rollout may take up to 2 weeks to complete.

**What is Gemini 4 Hydrogen?**
It's Google's new model for Search. Google calls it "the biggest model upgrade to Search yet" and says it is available globally in Google Search for all users.

**Why is November search data hard to read?**
The core update and Gemini 4 Hydrogen landed two days apart, so a change could come from either. Search Engine Journal notes that, on Google's two-week estimate, the first full week after the rollout has finished would include Black Friday, so compare like with like.

**Is Google sending more clicks from AI answers?**
Google says it has added more links to its AI experiences, but neither of its posts gives click numbers. Your own Search Console and analytics are the numbers to trust.
