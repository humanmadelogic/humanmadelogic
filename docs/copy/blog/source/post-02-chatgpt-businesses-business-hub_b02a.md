# You can now claim a ChatGPT business profile: what it means for small brands

By Rowan Blake
Reviewed by Kuntal Khanapurkar, Founder
Published: [TBC: publish date]

**The short answer.** Since 10 Nov 2026, eligible businesses can claim a ChatGPT business profile through OpenAI's Business Hub, now in beta. Once verified, you can update your details and see mentions and website clicks. Any eligible business where ChatGPT is available can claim, but Business Agents and shopping are US only, and claiming doesn't guarantee visibility.

## What happened

On 10 Nov 2026, OpenAI [opened Business Hub in beta](https://openai.com/index/introducing-businesses-chatgpt/). Eligible businesses can now claim a ChatGPT business profile, keep their details accurate and see how often ChatGPT mentions them and sends clicks to their site. To our eye, that's the closest thing yet to seeing your AI visibility from ChatGPT's own side.

Quick clarification, because the names are confusing: this isn't ChatGPT's paid Business plan. OpenAI's help page says ["You don't need an Ads account or a ChatGPT Enterprise agreement to claim an eligible business."](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001635-setting-up-your-business-on-chatgpt)

## What OpenAI actually launched

There are two parts, and only one of them matters to most UK founders right now.

**Business Hub** is the profile part. OpenAI says businesses can claim their organisation and, once verified, ["update key details and add self-service product feeds with pricing and availability"](https://openai.com/index/introducing-businesses-chatgpt/). It also gives you Insights: mentions, website clicks and discovery topics, which OpenAI describes as common themes across conversations where your website is cited ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-businesses-chatgpt/)).

**Business Agents** are a separate chat that answers customer questions using information you provide. OpenAI says ["Business Agents are now available to eligible ecommerce and online service businesses in the United States."](https://openai.com/index/introducing-businesses-chatgpt/) OpenAI's VP of monetisation, Benji Shomair, told Digiday the business agent is ["the organic experience"](https://digiday.com/marketing/openais-new-business-agents-give-brands-a-free-way-into-the-conversation-its-also-selling-as-an-ad/) and the sponsored version ["the paid experience"](https://digiday.com/marketing/openais-new-business-agents-give-brands-a-free-way-into-the-conversation-its-also-selling-as-an-ad/), and he declined to give a timeline for wider rollout. Reuters reported the same starting point, with OpenAI ["initially rolling out the feature to eligible e-commerce and online service businesses in the US"](https://www.reuters.com/5f374b32b07b/technology/openai-lets-businesses-claim-chatgpt-profiles-adds-ai-agents-us-e-commerce-firms-2026-11-10/).

Why OpenAI is pitching this hard: it says the beta is a new way to connect with ["the 1.2 billion people who use ChatGPT each week"](https://openai.com/index/introducing-businesses-chatgpt/). That's OpenAI's own worldwide figure, not a UK one.

## Can businesses in the UK, Australia, Canada and Singapore claim?

OpenAI's answer is: if you're eligible. It says ["Any eligible business located where ChatGPT is available can claim their business in ChatGPT through Business Hub."](https://openai.com/index/introducing-businesses-chatgpt/) Its help page adds that ["Availability depends on the business's location and type."](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001635-setting-up-your-business-on-chatgpt) OpenAI doesn't list the eligible countries or categories on these pages, so we won't promise it works in your market.

One catch for local businesses: if your listing doesn't exist yet, online businesses may see an option to add one, but OpenAI says ["Local stores and restaurants can currently claim only an existing U.S. listing."](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001635-setting-up-your-business-on-chatgpt)

What's US only for now: Business Agents. So is shopping in ChatGPT, which OpenAI's merchants page says is ["currently live for ChatGPT users in the U.S."](https://chatgpt.com/merchants/)

## What the mentions and clicks actually count

The numbers are useful, but they're counted on OpenAI's terms. Insights ["show aggregate activity over the past 28 days, including mentions and website clicks."](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001635-setting-up-your-business-on-chatgpt)

- **Mentions.** ["For an online business, a mention counts when ChatGPT references its website. Naming the brand alone doesn't count."](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001635-setting-up-your-business-on-chatgpt) For a local business, it counts when ChatGPT shows a result for that location.
- **Clicks.** An OpenAI spokesperson told Digiday: ["Website clicks are clicks on links from supported ChatGPT surfaces, not completed visits or purchases. And low-volume counts may be withheld for privacy."](https://digiday.com/marketing/openais-new-business-agents-give-brands-a-free-way-into-the-conversation-its-also-selling-as-an-ad/)
- **Discovery topics.** They're grouped themes, and businesses don't receive individual prompts or conversations ([OpenAI Help Center](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001635-setting-up-your-business-on-chatgpt)).
- **Blanks.** A blank metric ["doesn't necessarily mean zero activity."](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001635-setting-up-your-business-on-chatgpt)

Search Engine Journal suggests putting those clicks [next to the referrals your own analytics shows for the same 28 days](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/seo-pulse-core-update-chatgpt-business-hub-ai-modes-new-model/597135/). A blank template to copy:

|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## What claiming won't do

It won't buy you visibility. OpenAI is blunt about it: ["It doesn't guarantee increased visibility or give you direct control over ChatGPT's independent recommendations. Advertising doesn't influence ChatGPT's independent answers."](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001635-setting-up-your-business-on-chatgpt)

Shomair told Digiday claiming a business ["should not have an impact on how frequently it appears"](https://digiday.com/marketing/openais-new-business-agents-give-brands-a-free-way-into-the-conversation-its-also-selling-as-an-ad/), though he said clearer information could help ChatGPT surface a business in more relevant conversations. Business Hub and agents are free for now, and he didn't rule out a paid model later ([Digiday](https://digiday.com/marketing/openais-new-business-agents-give-brands-a-free-way-into-the-conversation-its-also-selling-as-an-ad/)).

So the profile is the admin. Being cited still comes down to clear, trustworthy information on your own site and elsewhere, which is what [our guide](/guides/how-to-get-your-brand-cited-by-chatgpt) on getting cited by ChatGPT covers step by step.

## What this means for your brand

1. **Claim it if you can, properly.** Verification options include phone, business email, website domain or Shopify, then an identity check with Persona, which may ask for government ID and business registration details ([OpenAI Help Center](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001635-setting-up-your-business-on-chatgpt)). Use the legal name on your registration documents, as OpenAI asks.
2. **Make the profile match everything else.** Once approved, you can update your website, description, category, logo, delivery policy and return policy ([OpenAI Help Center](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001635-setting-up-your-business-on-chatgpt)). We think it should say the same thing as your site and your LinkedIn page, word for word where you can.
3. **Read the 28 days, not the day.** Log the numbers monthly next to your own analytics and watch which discovery topics you show up for.
4. **Park agents and shopping until they reach you.** Both are US only today. If you're outside the US, the profile and the insights are the practical win.
5. **Give access, not logins.** The admin who claimed the business can add teammates and grant an agency access, and OpenAI says to use each person's own ChatGPT account ([OpenAI Help Center](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001635-setting-up-your-business-on-chatgpt)).
