# Google AI Mode ads are scaling in Australia, Canada and Singapore, with the UK planned

By Rowan Blake
Reviewed by Kuntal Khanapurkar, Founder
Published: [TBC: publish date]

**The short version:** Google is scaling ads in AI Mode in 10 new countries, including Australia, Canada and Singapore, and plans to add the UK in Q4 2026 ([Google, 28 Oct 2026](https://blog.google/products/ads-commerce/ads-in-ai-mode-global/)). If you sell there, AI answers are now a paid space as well as an organic one, so your pages and product data must hold up in both.

## What Google announced

On 28 October 2026, Shashi Thakur, Google's VP and GM for Search Ads and Ads on Google Experiences, wrote: "Now we're scaling ads in AI Mode in Australia, Canada, India, Kenya, Malaysia, New Zealand, Nigeria, Pakistan, the Philippines, and Singapore." ([Google](https://blog.google/products/ads-commerce/ads-in-ai-mode-global/))

The same post sets out what comes next: "In Q4 2026, we plan to expand to additional locales, including Brazil, Mexico, and the United Kingdom." ([Google](https://blog.google/products/ads-commerce/ads-in-ai-mode-global/)) That is a plan, not a launch. Google's post does not say ads in AI Mode are running in the UK, so neither do we.

Two smaller details are worth knowing. Google says "Ads will be clearly labeled as 'Sponsored.'" And it is "launching multiturn ads in the U.S." so ads can appear in follow-up questions ([Google](https://blog.google/products/ads-commerce/ads-in-ai-mode-global/)). That second one is US only for now.

## Where this leaves our four markets

- **Australia, Canada and Singapore:** on Google's list of countries where ads in AI Mode are scaling now ([Google](https://blog.google/products/ads-commerce/ads-in-ai-mode-global/)).
- **United Kingdom:** planned for Q4 2026, per the same post. Until Google confirms otherwise, plan as if it is coming, not as if it is here.

## The two numbers Google points to (both US)

Google gives two reasons it thinks ads fit in AI Mode. First: "In AI Mode, queries are, on average, three times longer than traditional searches." Google footnotes that to its "Google AI Mode Report, US, May 2026" ([Google](https://blog.google/products/ads-commerce/ads-in-ai-mode-global/)).

Second: "click-through rates are already similar between sponsored and organic product list items within AI Mode." The footnote reads "Google Internal Data, US, August 20, 2026- September 3, 2026" ([Google](https://blog.google/products/ads-commerce/ads-in-ai-mode-global/)).

Both are US figures, and both come from Google, which sells the ads. We'd read them as a sign of direction rather than a promise for your market.

## Organic still matters in the same answer

Two weeks earlier, Google said it had made AI answers more generous with links: "We've made continued and substantial increases to the presence of inline links in our AI experiences." It is also testing a display "to prominently surface these links in a dedicated section at the top of a response" for official websites ([Google, 15 Oct 2026](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/search/updates-ai-mode-overviews-explore-web/)).

To our eye, that is the real story. The sponsored card and the organic link now sit in the same answer, and in its US data Google says sponsored and organic product listings get similar click-through rates. A brand that is clear, credible and easy to cite gets a shot at both. A brand that only buys the ad is renting the space. Google's Ads Help page adds: "Paid advertising does not influence the organic responses generated by Google AI." ([Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/18826097))

## A note on timing

This is landing in a busy month. Google's November 2026 core update started rolling out on 10 November, and Google's Search Status Dashboard says it "may take up to 2 weeks to complete" ([Google Search Status Dashboard](https://status.search.google.com/incidents/WLxcJyC6jMWMfryxsKMc)). If your search traffic moves this month, don't pin it on AI Mode ads alone.

For UK context, search is already where most digital ad money goes. IAB UK's September 2026 study says: "Search remains the UK's largest digital advertising channel by some distance, reaching £9.1bn and accounting for 43% of the market." Those are first half 2026 figures for all search, not AI Mode ([IAB UK, 29 Sep 2026](https://www.iabuk.com/news-article/six-months-uk-digital-adspend-now-exceeds-whole-2020)). For the global picture, WARC's latest forecast says: "Global ad spend is forecast to grow 11.9% to $1.34trn in 2026". That is a global forecast, not a UK or AI Mode figure ([WARC, 7 Oct 2026](https://www.warc.com/en/article/global-ad-spend-outlook-q-3-2026-eb1a9505c8d34d1fb3833cf61f675165)).

## What Google says about eligibility

Google's Ads Help page says: "Existing Search, Shopping, and Performance Max campaigns are automatically eligible to serve ads within AI Overviews and AI Mode." It also says these ads are "available in English in the United States and select English-speaking markets" ([Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/18826097)). Check what applies to your own account and market before trusting any summary, including this one. We also won't suggest a budget. The right spend depends on your margins, your market and what you already run.

## What this means for your brand

1. **Selling in Australia, Canada or Singapore?** Ask whoever runs your Google Ads whether your campaigns can show in AI Mode, using [Google's Ads Help page](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/18826097) as your checklist.
2. **UK-first?** Treat this as coming, not live. Use the time to tidy your product data and landing pages so you're ready if Google confirms the UK launch.
3. **Pass the long-question test.** If queries really are longer in AI Mode (Google's US figure), make sure your pages answer specific, multi-part questions in plain English.
4. **Don't split paid and organic thinking.** Being the page Google links to and the ad it shows are two halves of the same answer. Our guide to [SEO, AEO and GEO](/guides/seo-vs-aeo-vs-geo) covers the organic half.
5. **Read November's numbers with care.** Google's November core update [started rolling out on 10 November](https://status.search.google.com/incidents/WLxcJyC6jMWMfryxsKMc), so compare like with like before you change anything.
